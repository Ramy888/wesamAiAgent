## What it does
1. **Price a product:** safe price, breakeven price, profit at your price, and the max cost
   per lead.
2. **Cost-per-lead table:** how much you can pay per lead at each margin.
3. **Bundles and offers:** 2/3/4-piece prices and "2 for X" checks before you launch them.
4. **Weekly campaign check:** PAUSE / FIX / SCALE from your real orders, with the one move
   that matters.
5. **Competitor check:** finds competitor offers, asks you to confirm them, and shows where
   your price stands.

## Why sellers trust it
- Every number comes from a tested calculation engine, never from AI guesswork.
- Returns, unconfirmed orders, shipping, fees and ads are all counted.
- Every assumption is stated; competitor prices come with links.
- Answers come with bilingual charts.
