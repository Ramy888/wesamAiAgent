What this seller does today

Prices by multiplying

Cost × 2.5 or 3, checked against whatever competitors are charging, and hope it covers ads and shipping.

Judges ads by ROAS

The platform reports revenue over spend. It cannot know what was confirmed, delivered, or sent back.

Adds a flat buffer for returns

A round number per order instead of the real cost of a failed delivery.

Each of those is defensible on its own. Together they hide the one number that decides the business: profit per delivered order.
