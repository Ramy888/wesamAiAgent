A cash-on-delivery seller cannot see their own profit

Half the orders never arrive

Leads must be confirmed on the phone, then delivered at the door. Returns cost shipping both ways, and the seller still paid for the ad.

The numbers live in four places

Ads Manager, the courier's sheet, the supplier invoice, and a spreadsheet that was last correct a month ago.

So the ad keeps running

A campaign that looks profitable in Ads Manager can lose money on every delivered order — and nobody notices for weeks.

Ads platforms report leads. They do not know which leads confirmed, which were delivered, or what the courier charged for the returns.

This is the problem slide. Judges outside e-commerce need the COD mechanics in one breath: you pay for the ad up front, you collect cash only if the customer answers the phone and opens the door.
